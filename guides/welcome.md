Mambo Database is a free, open-source library for designers. It has two goals.

1. **Help you build stronger foundations.** Good interface design rests on a few basics: accessible color, readable type, components that handle every state, and clean hand-off to engineers. Each entry here shows those basics done properly, so you can learn from them and reuse them.
2. **Help you understand how AI is being used in design.** AI tools now read, write and review design work. They do that best when your system is written down clearly. This site shows what "clearly" looks like, and gives AI tools the same structured files you read on screen.

## What is inside

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Browse | A searchable database of components, color sets and typefaces. Open any entry to see its anatomy, states, accessibility notes and code. |
| Design Systems | A workspace where you assemble colors, type, states and components into a system, with advice and contrast checks as you go. Everything is saved in your browser only. |
| Guides | Short lessons like this one: accessibility, HTML and CSS, hand-off, image formats and icons. |

## A good first ten minutes

1. Open **Browse** and click a component, such as Button. Look at the row of states from left to right. Every state is a screen your engineers need from you.
2. Open **Design Systems**, create a system, and read the setup checklist. Select an item to jump straight to it.
3. Read [Accessibility basics](#/guide/accessibility-basics). It changes how you pick colors.
4. Read [How AI reads a design system](#/guide/how-ai-reads-a-design-system) to see why structure matters.

## Is it free to use?

Yes. The code and original entries use the MIT license. Every typeface listed is open source under the SIL Open Font License, so it is free for commercial use. Credits are at the bottom of every page.

> **Good to know.** Mambo Database is a learning and starting-point tool. It gives well-reasoned defaults, but you and your team still decide what is right for your product and your users.
