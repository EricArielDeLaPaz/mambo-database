# Feeling to design direction

Starting points, not rules. Always check contrast afterwards.

| Feeling | Color direction | Type direction | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional | Navy or slate primary, lots of neutral | Clean sans (Inter, IBM Plex Sans) | Small radii |
| Trustworthy | Blue or deep teal, restrained accent | Humanist sans, readable body | Small radii, clear borders |
| Fun | Violet, coral or yellow accents on white | Rounded or geometric display (Fredoka, Plus Jakarta Sans) | Large radii |
| Calm | Soft teal or sage, warm neutrals | Light-to-regular weights, generous line height | Medium radii |
| Bold | Near-black with one saturated accent | Heavy display weights | Sharp or pill |
| Premium | Black, ivory, one muted metallic | Serif display (Playfair Display, Cormorant) with a clean sans | Small radii, lots of space |
| Friendly | Warm accent, soft neutrals | Rounded sans | Medium radii |
| Technical | Neutral grays, one electric accent | Sans with a mono (Space Mono, JetBrains Mono) | Small radii |
| Warm / appetizing | Reds, oranges, cream | Friendly serif or sans | Medium radii |
| Fresh | Green and white | Light geometric sans | Medium radii |

Industry hints: finance and legal lean blue and neutral; healthcare and wellness lean teal and green; food leans warm; kids and games allow bright palettes but still need 4.5:1 text.
