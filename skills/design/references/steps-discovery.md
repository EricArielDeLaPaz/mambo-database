# Discovery steps

Each card: purpose, what to ask, research type, what to write, when it is done. Number the output files in the order the steps run.

## problem-framing: Problem and goals
- **Purpose:** state the problem, who has it, and what success looks like, so later steps have one target.
- **Ask (one call):** who has the problem (free text); how they cope today (free text); the single outcome that matters most (3 options plus Other).
- **Research:** none.
- **Write:** problem statement (2 sentences), who, current workarounds, the outcome, what is out of scope, 3 assumptions to test.
- **Done when:** the person agrees the statement fits on one line.

## interviews: Interview plan (stakeholders and users)
- **Purpose:** decide who to talk to and what to learn; prepare the guide so the interviews can happen.
- **Ask:** who they can reach; how many conversations they can hold; the riskiest assumption.
- **Research:** none (the plan comes from the conversation). Offer web research only if the person wants help finding participants or channels.
- **Write:** 3 to 5 participant profiles, a recruiting message, a question guide (open questions, no leading ones), a note-taking template.
- **Done when:** the guide is ready to use. If interviews already happened, ask for the notes (paste or attach) and write a summary instead.

## competitive-audit: Competitive audit
- **Purpose:** see what people use today, where alternatives fall short, and where there is room.
- **Ask:** 3 to 6 known competitors or alternatives (including "doing it by hand"); what to compare (features, price, flows, tone).
- **Research:** web. Run the research handoff first. The brief asks for each product's audience, core flow, pricing, strengths, gaps, with a source URL per claim.
- **Write:** a comparison table, a gaps and opportunities list, a "do not copy" list, and what is unverified.
- **Done when:** every claim has a source or is marked unverified.

## users-jtbd: Users and jobs to be done
- **Purpose:** turn research into the jobs people are hiring the product to do.
- **Ask:** whether to include personas (offer light personas, optional).
- **Research:** internal (uses the earlier outputs and the person's notes); offer to import notes.
- **Write:** 3 to 6 jobs in the form "When <situation>, I want to <motivation>, so I can <outcome>", their frequency and importance, and one light persona per main user if chosen.
- **Done when:** each job traces to something the person said or a note they supplied.
